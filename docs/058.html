Editorial notes

  • Each gotra table runs across a pair of facing pages: this page gives सं०, गोत्र, आस्पद, प्रवर and स्थान, and the facing page (गोत्रावली) gives the पंक्ति, शाखा, सूत्र, वेद, उपवेद, शिखा, पाद and देवता of the same rows. In print the प्रवर names and the प्रसिद्ध स्थान run down beside the rows; here each cell lists them one per line. „ is the printed ditto mark.
  • Place names broken across printed lines with a hyphen are joined (e.g. ‘असौंजी नगरा’, ‘राउतपार’, ‘मिश्रौलिया’, ‘वढ़यापार’).
